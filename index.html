How fills work

Bar replay fills orders at the bar close and checks stops and targets on later bars. If both are inside one bar, the stop is assumed first.
Live ticks builds each candle tick by tick from the lower-timeframe bars (e.g. a 15m candle from its 1m bars), one tick per second at most. Orders fill at the current tick, and stops and targets fill at their exact level. Gaps aren't modelled as slippage.
Indicators use only bars already revealed, so there's no look-ahead.
